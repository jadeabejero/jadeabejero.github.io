30-Day Content Calendar — Real Estate Agent

A month of posts planned, drafted, and scheduled for a solo realtor: 4 posts a week, Canva templates that stay on-brand, and a monthly analytics report that decides next month's plan.

SCENARIO: Solo realtor, Facebook + Instagram, no posting rhythm TOOLS: Meta Business Suite · Canva · Sheets DELIVERABLE: Calendar + 16 drafted posts + report

1 · Content pillars — the rhythm behind the calendar

LISTING SPOTLIGHT — Mon BUYER TIP TUESDAY BEHIND-THE-SCENES — Thu MARKET SNAPSHOT — Fri

Four repeating pillars means the client never stares at a blank feed, and every post has a job: attract, teach, trust-build, or prove expertise.

4 · The working system

  • Batch day (1st Monday): all visuals for the month built in Canva in one 3-hour session
  • Copy bank: 30+ caption hooks per pillar — no post starts from a blank page
  • Scheduling: everything queued a week ahead; posting never depends on the client's mood or memory
  • Comment duty: comments & DMs answered twice daily within business hours — leads never sit overnight
  • Monthly: analytics report + a 15-min call to agree on next month's tweaks
Outcome

A realtor who hadn't posted in 2 months now has a consistent, on-brand feed where every post has a purpose — and DM inquiries more than doubled by month two.
