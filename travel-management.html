3 · Booked & confirmed checklist

  • Flights — seats assigned, online check-in reminder set 24h before both legs
  • Hotel — early check-in + late checkout confirmed in writing, not just requested
  • Transport — airport pickups and meeting-day driver booked with contact numbers in itinerary
  • Meetings — client confirmations in calendar, invites updated with building + parking notes
  • Day before: reconfirm everything, check weather, send founder the final one-page itinerary
Outcome — why this wins

The founder touches nothing except picking A or B. Every leg has a driver's number, every meeting has a printed backup, every risk has a plan B — and the expense report is basically done before the trip ends.
