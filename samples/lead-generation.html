Lead Gen Sprint — Dental Clinic Outreach

A one-week prospecting sprint for a dental-supply vendor: 40 qualified clinics researched, a personalized email sequence written, and a tracked pipeline the sales team can work from Monday morning.

SCENARIO: Supplier wants new clinic accounts in Metro Manila TOOLS: Sheets · Maps · email tracking DELIVERABLE: 40-lead list + sequence + pipeline

1 · Qualifying rules — who makes the list

2–10 chair clinics (right size to buy) QC · Caloocan · Pasig — within service radius Active practice: bookings visible, staff present Decision-maker identified where possible

40 clinics passed; 23 more were scanned and rejected with reasons logged — quality beats a fat list nobody answers.

3 · The outreach sequence (3 touches, 8 days)

TOUCH 1 — Day 1, personalized opener Subject: equipping the new Pasig branch?

Hi Dr. Reyes — congrats on the Pasig branch opening; saw the photos of the new operatories on your page.

We supply clinics your size with chairside consumables at 10–15% below list when ordered monthly. Want me to send the price sheet for the items you'll burn through fastest?

Why it works: specific to them, one clear ask, no attachment spam on first touch
TOUCH 2 — Day 4, value addif no reply

Dr. Reyes — one thing clinics switching to us mention most: no minimum order on the first three months, so you can match orders to the branch's actual patient flow while it ramps up.

Short, one new fact, zero guilt-tripping
TOUCH 3 — Day 8, graceful closebreakup email

Hi again — I'll stop reaching out so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If equipment or supply costs become a topic later, the price sheet is here anytime: [link]. Wishing the new branch a strong opening.

Breakup emails get surprising replies — polite door left open
