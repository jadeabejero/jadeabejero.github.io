Research Brief — Co-working Space Shortlist

An open question turned into a decision-ready shortlist: 12 providers scanned, 6 evaluated in depth, one clear recommendation with the math shown.

SCENARIO: Startup needs a 6-person team home in Metro Manila TOOLS: Google Sheets · official pricing pages · calls DELIVERABLE: Comparison matrix + recommendation

1 · The question, sharpened

Client asked: "find us a co-working space." Vague briefs produce vague answers, so I confirmed constraints first:

6 desks, 12-month commit Budget ≤ ₱9,000/desk/mo BGC or Makati CBD Meeting room ≥ 8h/mo included 24/7 access required

3 · Recommendation

Go with Provider A — here's the math on a 6-desk, 12-month deal:

  • ₱590,400/yr list price — negotiated to ₱492,000 using the 2-months-free annual promo
  • ₱98,400 saved vs. list — vs. ₱26,400 saved at runner-up B
  • Meets all 5 hard requirements, and the quiet floor matters for client calls
  • Risk noted: only 4 desks free now; I secured a written commitment for 2 more by Nov 1
  • If A falls through: Provider F's waitlist — I'd place the deposit the same day

4 · Verification trail

  • Every price checked against the provider's official pricing page, dated in the sheet
  • Ambiguous items resolved by phone calls — names and dates logged in the notes column
  • Third-party reviews scanned for red flags (billing surprises, security incidents): none found
  • Refresh cycle: quotes expire — sheet re-verified every 30 days while decision is open
Outcome

The client read one page, saw one recommendation, and signed. Total research time: 1.5 days — cheaper than the ₱98,400 it saved.
