Household Bills & Services Tracker

A private-client system for running a household the way an office is run: every bill on a schedule, every provider on file, every renewal caught before it lapses — with nothing sensitive ever exposed.

SCENARIO: Overseas client, PH property + family bills TOOLS: Google Sheets · GCash · calendar reminders DELIVERABLE: Bills tracker + provider directory

2 · The three rules that keep this safe

  • Client's money never sits with me: payments run from the client's own GCash/wallet, I execute and file proof
  • Nothing sensitive stored: no card numbers, no passwords — tracker holds due dates and status, nothing else
  • Two-step verification: every bill marked paid only after the confirmation screenshot is filed in Drive

4 · Renewal & maintenance radar

  • Annual renewals calendared: fire insurance (Feb), condo parking lease (Jun) — never caught late
  • Seasonal maintenance: aircon serviced before summer, gutters before rainy season
  • Bill anomalies flagged: electric bill 30% over 3-month average → I ask why before paying
  • Weekly (15 min): sweep upcoming dues, confirm last week's payments filed, ping client only if a decision is needed
Outcome

No late fees since the tracker started, renewals caught 30 days early, and the client — overseas and busy — gets one tidy monthly summary instead of a dozen anxious "did this get paid?" messages.
