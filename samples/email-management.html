Inbox Management Sprint

A demonstration inbox taken from 0 unread to zero-and-kept-there: triage rules, pre-drafted replies, and a daily sweep routine an executive can rely on without checking in.

SCENARIO: Busy founder, 214 unread emails TOOLS: Gmail · Filters · Templates TIMEFRAME: One working day, then 20 min/day

1 · The starting mess

A realistic founder inbox: newsletters mixed with client emergencies, invoices nobody answered, and meeting requests buried three days deep. Nothing was labeled; urgent items sat beside promotions from 2024.

214 unread 9 unanswered client emails 2 unpaid invoices overdue 0 labels · 0 filters

2 · Triage rules I set up

Rule set — applied via Gmail filters, maintained weekly
CategoryDetection ruleActionResponse SLA

Invoices / billing“invoice”, “payment due”, accounting domainLabel “Finance”, forward to bookkeeperSame day

3 · Draft replies — written to sound like the executive, not an assistant

DRAFT — to client (unanswered 6 days) RE: Proposal revision — Northwind

Hi Dana,

Thanks for your patience — I wanted to give your feedback proper attention rather than a rushed reply. The revised proposal now reflects the reduced scope we discussed: onboarding covers one product line instead of three, which brings the total to $18,500 and shortens delivery to six weeks.

If that works, I can have the contract over to you today.

Sent on behalf of the founder · Approved before sending · Draft saved with context notes
SENT — vendor escalation RE: Invoice #1042 — payment not received

Hello Maria,

Checking in on invoice #1042 ($2,300, due Sep 12). I understand it may have slipped during your system migration — could you confirm the payment date? Happy to resend the invoice if it's easier.

Appreciate it — we'd like to keep the monthly cadence uninterrupted on our side too.

Resolved: payment scheduled Sep 19 · Follow-up reminder set

4 · Daily sweep checklist

  • 08:30 — First pass: flag anything from clients or finance before the founder wakes up fully
  • 12:00 — Midday: reply to scheduling requests using calendar's real availability, never double-book
  • 16:30 — Sweep: everything actioned, archived, or converted into a task with a due date
  • 17:00 — Handoff note to founder: 3 bullets max — what needs their eyes, what I already handled
  • Fri 15:00 — Weekly: clear “Reading” digest, prune stale filters, report inbox stats
Outcome — what this routine delivers

Day one: 214 → 0 unread, 9 client emails answered, both invoices moving again. Steady state: the founder spends ~10 minutes a day on email instead of an hour, and nothing client-facing waits more than two hours.
