Executive Calendar Rebuild

A chaotic executive week rebuilt into a protected, color-coded schedule — meeting intake, conflict resolution, focus blocks, and a briefing sheet delivered every morning.

SCENARIO: Founder, 40+ meetings/week, constant double-booking TOOLS: Google Calendar · Calendly rules · Docs DELIVERABLE: Weekly template + morning briefing

1 · Problems found in the audit

6 double-bookings in one week No focus time — deep work erased by fragments 19 min average gap between calls — no buffer Timezone errors: 3 meetings set in wrong TZ

2 · The weekly template I designed

Recurring structure — colors match the executive's actual calendar
BlockTime (Mon–Fri)Purpose / rules
Deep Work07:30–10:00 dailyNo meetings accepted. Declines auto-include two alternative slots.
Team & standups10:00–12:00Internal only, 25-min default, 5-min buffer built in.
Client window13:00–16:00External calls. Booking link opens only these slots.
Admin & buffer16:00–17:00Overflow, follow-ups, next-day prep. Absorbs overruns.
Friday PM14:00 onwardWeekly review + next-week lock-in. No external meetings.

3 · Conflict resolution — how I handle collisions

  • Revenue first: client meetings outrank internal ones; I move internals, never externals
  • Never cancel silently: the moved party gets an apology + two new slot options the same hour
  • Travel math: any in-person meeting gets drive time padded both sides — enforced, not suggested
  • Timezone guard: every invite checked against the guest's city, not just mine — DST included
  • Standing rule: no-meeting Wednesday mornings — protected for the founder's reviews

4 · Morning briefing sheet (delivered 07:00 daily)

Wed — 3 external, 2 internal, 1 decision needed

• 09:00 Northwind renewal call — they hinted at budget cut; brief attached (last quarter's usage numbers)

• 13:00 Design review — deck v3 in Drive, two slides flagged for your call

• 15:30 New hire chat — HR sent questions; you approved them Tuesday

• DECISION 17:00 vendor quote expires today — my recommendation in your email, one-word reply is enough

Outcome — what the executive feels

Zero double-bookings since the template landed. Meetings cluster into coherent blocks, afternoons get 10 protected deep-work hours a week, and every morning starts with a one-glance briefing instead of calendar archaeology.
