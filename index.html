"I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." — Philippians 4:13

For years, this verse sat on a small notepad at my workstation — something I read before every shift. I spent my entire college life as a working student: construction work, a stint exploring trading, and eventually a career in BPO, all while pursuing a full-time Civil Engineering degree.

Balancing both took more than time — it took discipline and sacrifice. There were shifts where I handled clients solo while the rest of my team was on a different schedule, because my leaders trusted me enough to accommodate my class schedule. That trust, and the support of my family, is a big part of why I show up the way I do at work today.

In 2026, I graduated with a B.S. in Civil Engineering, majoring in Construction Engineering and Management — a milestone that represents years of long shifts, sleepless nights, and choosing not to quit when it would've been easier to.
