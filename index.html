  1. Umbra

    Desktop app · Electron

    Your private notepad for presenting. It stays on top of your screen but hidden from screen share, so you can keep your talking points in front of you while the audience sees only your slides. Ask it anything and it answers from your own notes, with optional AI drafting on your own key. Everything stays in one local file: no account, no tracking.

  2. MarginX

    Desktop app · Python

    An AI notepad for meetings. It records the call, enhances your rough notes, pulls out action items into a task list, keeps a job tracker and writes weekly digests. Claude can read the notes through MCP.

  3. KB Overlay

    Desktop app · Python

    A live answer desk for support calls. It transcribes the customer, matches the conversation against an SOP and QA knowledge base, and shows the best answer in a small overlay, with an optional AI rewrite and a session summary at the end.
